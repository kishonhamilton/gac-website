What's Up

G5

G5 is our youth ministry — a space for teens to wrestle with real questions, build real friendships, and grow a faith that's their own. Through honest teaching, worship, and community, we want every teen to know they belong here and that God has a purpose for their life.

Whether you're new to church or you've grown up in one, G5 is a place to show up as you are.
