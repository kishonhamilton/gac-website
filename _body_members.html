Member Login

Sign in with your church member account to access your dashboard.

For the developer This page is a functional placeholder. "Sign In" calls your backend at GET /api/auth/login, which redirects to the church's official ChMS member login page. The Pushpay ChMS v1 API this site integrates with is a Basic-Auth service account with no OAuth2/SSO flow, so this site cannot authenticate an individual member itself — CCB/ChMS remains the sole authentication authority, and members sign in on ChMS's own site. See server/src/routes/auth.js and README.md.

Future Scope

A Site-Hosted Member Dashboard

The cards below sketch a possible future member dashboard hosted on this site. They are not currently buildable with the confirmed ChMS API (a shared Basic-Auth service account with no per-member login) — they'd require a separate, real per-member identity system layered on top. Today, signed-in members simply use ChMS's own site for their profile, groups, and events.
