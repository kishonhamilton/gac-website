Welcome To

GAC Kids

GAC Kids is our children's ministry — a place where kids can grow in their faith through age-appropriate Bible teaching, worship, and play. Every Sunday, our caring teachers and volunteers create a warm, secure environment where children build a foundation of faith and make friends along the way.

We believe children can encounter God in a real way, and we're committed to making that encounter fun, safe, and memorable for every family who walks through our doors.

What to Expect

A Sunday with GAC Kids

Bible Teaching

Engaging, age-appropriate Bible lessons that bring Scripture to life and help kids build a real relationship with God.

Games & Activities

Supervised games, crafts, and hands-on activities in a safe environment where kids make friends and have fun.

Worship & Praise

Kid-friendly worship songs that let children praise God with their whole hearts — singing, clapping, and celebrating together.
