Invest in the Kingdom.

Every gift you bring reaches our campuses, our community, and people we haven't met yet. Here's how to give — and where it goes.

“Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.”

2 Corinthians 9:7

Our Goal Is Simple

Reach, Teach, and Win

Every gift helps us do what we've always been about — reaching people with the Gospel, teaching the Word of God, and winning souls for the Kingdom.

Reach

Local outreach and missions support that puts the Gospel in front of our neighbors — in Mount Vernon, Williamsbridge, Lithonia, and beyond.

Teach

Bible classes, discipleship resources, and ministry to every generation, from GAC Kids to G5 to our seasoned saints.

Win

Facilities, staff, and services that keep the doors open and the altar ready for every soul who walks through them.
